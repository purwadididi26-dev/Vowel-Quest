IPA Explorer: Sound Archive

How to use: pick a group, tap a sound to open its card, then listen. ✦ marks sounds you have collected through quizzes. The inventory is traditional British English RP: 12 monophthongs, 8 diphthongs and 24 consonants.

Academic note: /ʊə/ is not a distinct diphthong for all modern speakers, and RP is non-rhotic: /r/ is pronounced only before a vowel. For the vowel chart diagrams, see the Vowel Chart.
