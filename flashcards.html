Flashcards

How to use: pick a collection. Look at the symbol, say the sound, then tap the card to flip it. Use Previous and Next to move and Shuffle to mix the cards. Mark a card Mastered or Still learning. Review Again opens your Review collection: cards marked Still learning plus sounds you got wrong in quizzes. Your choices are saved in this browser.


Flashcards use the traditional RP teaching inventory (12 monophthongs, 8 diphthongs). Audio is a recording only if you have added verified files; otherwise use the browser voice, which is not authentic RP.
